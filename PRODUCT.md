# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Institutional owners (government agencies, public authorities, REITs, institutional developers), public agency directors, mid-sized general contractors, construction sureties, construction lenders, and construction-litigation counsel operating in the NJ/NY metropolitan region. They are financially sophisticated and responsible for capital projects ranging from $30M to $1B+. They arrive at this site when a specific high-stakes decision is forcing the issue: procurement sign-off, contingency approval, a project trending negative, or litigation positioning. They are skeptical of credentials and respond to demonstrated methodology.

## Product Purpose

Travo (Total Risk Analysis and Value Optimization) is a specialty quantitative construction risk advisory practice. It replaces intuition-driven contingency and color-coded heat maps with defensible, probabilistically derived decisions through Monte Carlo analysis. The website's job is to establish Travo as the authoritative regional standard — not to explain what risk advisory is. Success is a sophisticated buyer requesting a conversation, or storing Travo as the firm to call when the next complex capital project begins.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous · Independent · Authoritative. Trust earned through demonstrated precision, not through warmth or likability. The brand voice is expert and direct — the way a senior engineer writes a report, not the way a consulting firm writes a brochure. Travo demonstrates methodology rather than markets credentials.

## Anti-references

- **Generic management consultancies** (Deloitte, KPMG, Accenture): corporate navy, stock photo handshakes, safe and forgettable. Signals "one of many" rather than "the standard."
- **Warm artisan agency aesthetic**: cream/sand/paper backgrounds, earthy roundness, lifestyle warmth. Signals soft, not rigorous. The current codebase's exact palette.
- **Startup SaaS / risk-tech**: gradient dashboards, animated hero metrics, purple UI, breathless copy. Too product-y for a boutique analytical advisory. Reads as vendor, not authority.
- **Finance-bro luxury**: black with gold accents, wealth-management energy. Flash over substance.

## Organizing Idea

**"The Distribution / Quantifying the Unseen."** The Gaussian distribution curve is the firm's central symbol: it represents the shift from a single-point estimate (the industry default) to a full probability distribution (the Travo methodology). This symbol should thread through every section where data appears.

## Key Research Data

Capital project outcomes (industry benchmark, N≈10,000 projects):
- **8%** finish under budget
- **22%** finish on budget
- **28%** overrun by less than 30%
- **42%** overrun by 30% or more

Summary: 70% overrun. More than half of those by 30% or more. This data is used in the CoreBelief section.

## Design Principles

1. **Demonstrate, don't claim.** Every section should show methodology rather than state credentials. Data visualizations, probability ranges, structured catalogs with defined parameters — the design itself signals how the firm thinks.
2. **Authority through precision.** Spacing, type sizing, alignment, and color should all feel engineered. Precision in the design signals precision in the analysis.
3. **Bounded and deliberate.** The firm is "deliberately bounded." Nothing in the design should feel decorative or gratuitous. Every element earns its place.
4. **The standard, not a vendor.** The visual language should feel like it belongs to the firm setting the regional standard — not pitching to join it.
5. **Dark conviction.** The dark navy surface is not aesthetic preference; it is positioning. Precision instruments, trading terminals, and engineering environments run dark. Travo belongs in that company.
6. **The risk spectrum encodes meaning.** The five-stop gradient (dark teal → tidal aqua → air blue → blush → coral) is the firm's data language. Every visualization uses it semantically: teal for managed/positive outcomes, coral for critical/overrun. The spectrum is not decorative — it is diagnostic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA throughout. Dark-background text must achieve ≥4.5:1 contrast. The teal accent on dark surfaces must be verified on all surface variants. All animations require a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative.
